BURST

Yard

The yard hides five buried things. Uncover each one and press E to match its pipes, then turn that supply on at the house. Fire your ground probe (F or right-click): it reports the distance and depth of the nearest object and draws a ring on the lawn. Probe from several spots and look where rings cross. Dig there. Space jumps, and you walk up a low step. Once the water rises you can swim out of a deep hole. Don't hit the gas line. Digging also turns up trash, rocks and artifacts; rarer finds sit deeper. Trash goes in the trashcan, rocks and artifacts in the crate. A plain rock is worth 1 point. E scoops every small plain rock in reach, up to 8, so one trip to the crate or the trashcan banks the pile. The shovel stops at the stone about 3.5 m down. Buy a Pickaxe at the workbench to break it and reach the leak. Water rises from the bedrock: shut the leak before the yard floods. Buy a Pump at the workbench, then press E at the yellow pump by the house (or P) to drain the holes. Press B for your collection book. Rings fade after a few seconds. Only when you're deep in a hole right next to the leak will you hear a faint hiss. The water keeps rising until its supply switch on the house is on. The yard is finished when all five supplies are on. Until then, you can keep digging.

Yard

HOW TO PLAY

  1. Mow a patch of lawn. The shovel only digs grass that has been cut, and it stops at the stone. Buy a Pickaxe at the workbench to break through.
  2. Probe with F or right-click. A ring on the lawn is the distance to the nearest buried thing.
  3. Probe from a few spots, then dig where the rings cross.
  4. Five things are buried and broken. Uncover each one, then press E to repair it. Don't hit the gas line before you mean to.
  5. Water rises from the bedrock until the water supply is on. Space swims when you are in the water. Buy a Pump, then E at the yellow pump by the house (or P) to drain it.
  6. After a repair, aim at its switch on the house front and press E. The yard is done when all five supplies are on.

Walk with W A S D, sprint with Shift, jump with Space. Left click digs. E picks up whatever you aim at. One press scoops up to 8 small plain rocks. Trash goes in the trashcan. Rocks and artifacts go in the crate. B opens your collection. Esc pauses.

If the mouse doesn't capture, drag to look and click to dig.
