BURST

The yard hides five buried things — and only one is the leaking main. Fire your ground probe (F or right-click): it reports the distance and depth of the nearest object and draws a ring on the lawn. Probe from several spots and look where rings cross. Dig there — soil slides, so deep holes must be wide. Old tanks and lines are decoys. Digging also turns up trash and artifacts. Carry trash to the trashcan and artifacts to the wooden crate for points. Rings fade after a few seconds. Only when you're deep in a hole right next to the leak will you hear a faint hiss.
